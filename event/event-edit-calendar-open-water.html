View or Edit an Open Water Calendar Listing Event

You can edit or view your calendar listing details or you can upgrade to a sanction or recognition. If you choose to upgrade, you can edit any of your existing information and will need to add additional information to complete the sanction process. If you choose to edit, you can make changes to any section except for Event Type. If you make an edit, you must click Save & Continue to proceed to the next section. Once you have finished editing, click Save & Submit.
